TAVRICON — Blog Page A.C.T.D.F. Specification
A — ACT

The Blog will serve all major purposes:

Educate visitors
Establish TAVRICON as an authority
Generate SEO traffic
Attract potential clients
Keep visitors engaged

My role will be all of the above, with Digital Marketing Expert as the primary perspective.

C — CONTEXT

The content will serve all target audiences—from beginners and creators to startups, e-commerce businesses, established companies, Indian/local businesses, and international clients.

The content identity will be a combination of:

Practical strategies
Easy-to-understand explanations
Data/research
Real-world marketing
AI and future-focused insights
Strong expert opinions

The key is that TAVRICON should sound like an agency that actually understands marketing, not a website generating generic SEO articles.

T — TASK

The Blog will cover all relevant categories:

SEO • Social Media • Google Ads • Meta Ads • Performance Marketing • Content Marketing • Email Marketing • AI Marketing • Automation • Branding • Business Growth • Digital Marketing Guides • Case Studies • Marketing Trends • TAVRICON Updates • Founder/Entrepreneurship

Article discovery will use a combination of:

Featured article
Categories
Search
Latest articles
Popular articles
Recommended articles
D — DETAILING

The Blog will retain the TAVRICON premium visual language, combining:

Deep Royal + Modern Premium + Editorial + Futuristic + Sophisticated Organic/Tropical

The article cards will use a combination of:

Large featured content
Premium editorial cards
Strong imagery
Category labels
Clear typography
Short descriptions
Subtle hover interactions
Controlled animations

Individual articles will have a polished reading experience with a combination of:

Large hero
Author
Date
Reading time
Table of contents
Related articles
Social sharing
Newsletter/lead capture
Relevant service CTA
F — FORMAT

The conversion path will remain natural rather than aggressive:

Discover article → Read → Gain value → Trust TAVRICON → Explore relevant service → Contact / Consultation

The Blog shouldn't scream “BUY OUR SERVICES.”

Instead, a reader should finish an article thinking:

“These guys actually know what they're talking about.”

And then naturally move toward TAVRICON's services or contact page.
