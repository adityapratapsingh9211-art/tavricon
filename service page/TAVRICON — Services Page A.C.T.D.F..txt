TAVRICON — Services Page A.C.T.D.F. Specification
A — ACT
Role: Digital marketing expert + strategist + conversion-focused UX designer.
Purpose: Explain TAVRICON's capabilities, demonstrate expertise, build trust, and convert visitors into potential clients.
C — CONTEXT
Target audience: International businesses, Indian businesses, Jaipur/local businesses, startups, e-commerce brands, creators and personal brands.
Positioning: TAVRICON should present its services as premium, modern, growth-focused, customized, and technology-driven rather than simply listing services.
T — TASK

The page will contain these 8 core services:

SEO
Social Media Marketing
Performance Marketing / PPC
Google Ads
Meta Ads
Content Marketing
Email Marketing
AI Marketing & Automation

The services will initially appear together on one Services page, but each service should have enough information to understand:

What it is
What problem it solves
What TAVRICON does
Key benefits
How the service contributes to growth
A clear CTA

For Google Ads and Meta Ads, I recommend presenting them as specialized capabilities under the broader Performance Marketing system visually, while still giving them their own service cards. That makes the offering look strategically structured rather than repetitive.

D — DETAILING

The page will follow the established TAVRICON visual identity:

Deep royal + modern premium + sophisticated tropical + futuristic elements.

I'll use a combination of:

Premium service cards
Subtle glass/solid surfaces
Abstract marketing/data graphics
Elegant gradients
Strategic motion
Hover interactions
Scroll-based reveals
Numbers/data-style visual elements
Strong typography hierarchy
Slightly rounded premium buttons

Animation should feel expensive and intentional, not like a template full of effects.

The service cards can have different visual personalities while still belonging to the same design system. The most important services can receive stronger visual emphasis.

F — FORMAT

The Services page should ultimately guide visitors through:

Discover → Understand → Trust → Explore → Enquire

Recommended structure:

1. Services Hero
A strong hook such as:

Marketing built to move your business forward.

Supporting copy + primary CTA + sophisticated animated visual.

2. TAVRICON's Growth Approach
A short explanation that we don't simply sell isolated marketing services—we combine the right channels around the client's growth objective.

3. All Services
The eight services displayed in a premium interactive grid.

4. Individual Service Deep-Dives
SEO → Social Media → Performance Marketing → Google Ads → Meta Ads → Content → Email → AI & Automation.

5. How We Build Your Strategy
Discover → Strategy → Execute → Optimize → Scale.

6. Why TAVRICON
Premium quality + customized strategy + modern technology + growth focus.

7. FAQ

8. Final Conversion Section

Ready to turn attention into growth?

With options such as:

Start a Conversation
Request a Proposal
Book a Consultation

The page's primary psychological takeaway should be:

“TAVRICON doesn't just offer marketing services. They understand how these services work together to grow a business.”S
