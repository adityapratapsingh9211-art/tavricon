D — DETAILING

The Contact page will maintain the established TAVRICON deep-royal + premium + sophisticated tropical + modern/futuristic identity.

I'll use a simple traditional form, but make the surrounding experience premium through:

Strong typography
Elegant spacing
Premium input fields
Subtle animation
Sophisticated background graphics
Clear CTA
Trust elements
A concise personal message from Aditya Singh
Selected social/contact information
Small FAQ section where useful

I would not put a huge map or unnecessary office imagery on the page unless TAVRICON actually has a physical office you want clients to visit. For an international-facing agency, that space is better used for conversion and credibility.
