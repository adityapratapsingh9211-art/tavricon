Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2025 • Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

TAVRICON (“we”, “our”, or “agency”) is committed to protecting your privacy and treating your data with absolute transparency. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, handle, store, and protect information when you visit our website, submit proposal requests, or engage our digital marketing services.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to communicate effectively and deliver high-yield marketing strategies:

  • Inquiry Data: When you complete our proposal or contact form, we collect your name, business email address, phone/WhatsApp number, company name, website URL, budget preferences, and project goals.
  • Technical Data: Standard internet log information including browser type, operating system, pages viewed, and session duration to ensure site security and optimize user experience.
  • Client Assets: Once engaged as a client, we receive access credentials to ad managers, Google Analytics, and marketing platforms strictly to execute contracted campaigns.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively for legitimate business purposes:

  • To evaluate your marketing needs and prepare customized proposal roadmaps.
  • To respond to your inquiries via email, phone, or WhatsApp.
  • To execute, optimize, and report on digital marketing campaigns.
  • To dispatch our strategic newsletter if you have explicitly opted in.

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal or business data to third-party brokers or advertisers.

4. Cookies & Analytics

Our website uses privacy-conscious cookies and modern analytics tools (such as Google Analytics and Meta Pixel) to understand site traffic and improve performance. You can disable cookies at any time through your browser settings without impacting your access to our core website content.

5. Data Security & Storage

We implement industry-standard encryption, secure cloud infrastructure, and strict role-based access protocols to protect your personal information against unauthorized disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your information.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time.
